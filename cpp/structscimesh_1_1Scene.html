Detailed Description

A collection of Mesh objects to be rendered together.

A Scene is simply a list of meshes. When rendered, meshes are drawn in order — later meshes appear on top of earlier ones.

Construction

Scene scene;
scene.meshes.push_back(sphere);
scene.meshes.push_back(cube);
scene.meshes.push_back(arrow);

Compute the combined axis-aligned bounding box of all meshes.

Iterates over all meshes and computes the union of their individual bounding boxes. Empty meshes are skipped.

Parameters
[out]min_boundMinimum corner of the combined AABB.
[out]max_boundMaximum corner of the combined AABB.
Example
Vec3 bmin, bmax;
scene.compute_bounding_box(bmin, bmax);
std::cout << "Scene spans " << (bmax - bmin) << std::endl;
