Detailed Description

Shared font state: the font bytes, the parsed font and the glyph cache.

Held by std::shared_ptr so that copying a Font is cheap and all copies share the rasterized glyphs. The raw bytes are kept alive because stbtt_fontinfo stores pointers into them.
