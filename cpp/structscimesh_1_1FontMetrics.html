Detailed Description

Vertical metrics of a font at a given pixel size.

All values are distances in output pixels. They describe a single line of text: the baseline sits at some pixel row, ascent pixels above it is the top of the tallest glyph, descent pixels below it is the lowest point of the line (positive value), and line_gap is the recommended additional spacing between two lines.
